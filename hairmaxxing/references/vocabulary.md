# Vocabulary

Use these terms with the user and translate them into physical descriptions in prompts. Image models follow measurements and anatomical anchors far better than style names.

## Sides and back

| Term | Prompt description |
|---|---|
| Taper | Only the sideburns and the neckline are faded short; the rest of the sides keeps hair. |
| Low fade | Shortest just above the ear and along the neckline; above the top of the ear the sides stay dark with hair. |
| Mid fade | Shortest up to about the top of the ear, blending up to the temples. |
| High fade | Short up to the temples, where the head starts to round; very little length on the sides. |
| Skin (bald) fade | Any of the above, but the lowest band is bare skin. State the height of the bare band. |
| Drop fade | The fade line curves down behind the ear toward the nape. |
| Burst fade | A semicircle fade around the ear; the back keeps length. |
| Temple fade | Only the temples and sideburns are faded, usually with a line-up. |
| Undercut | Sides short at one length with a hard disconnect from a much longer top. |
| Scissor cut | No clippers; sides a few cm long and blended into the top. |

Clipper guards: #0 = 0 mm, #0.5 = 1.5 mm, #1 = 3 mm, #1.5 = 4.5 mm, #2 = 6 mm, #3 = 10 mm, #4 = 13 mm, #5 = 16 mm, #6 = 19 mm, #8 = 25 mm.

Necklines: tapered (fades to skin), blocked (squared straight line), rounded.

## Top

- **Buzz cut:** one short length all over. **Crew cut:** short, slightly longer at the front.
- **Caesar:** short, even length with a short straight fringe brushed forward.
- **Ivy League:** short crew with enough length on top to side-part.
- **Textured crop:** 3-6 cm, choppy, pushed forward, short fringe.
- **French crop:** like a textured crop with a longer, straighter fringe.
- **Quiff:** volume at the front, swept up and back.
- **Pompadour:** longer top swept back with clear height at the front, smooth.
- **Slick back:** longer top combed straight back, flat, often with shine.
- **Side part / comb over:** combed to one side from a defined part; a hard part is shaved in.
- **Curtains:** middle part, fringe falling to both sides of the forehead.
- **Flow:** 10-15 cm, pushed back, ends falling behind the ears.
- **Modern mullet:** short sides, length kept at the back. **Wolf cut:** shaggy and layered all over.

## Curly and coily hair

- **Curly top fade:** defined curls on top, any fade on the sides. Name the curl type (2A-4C) and the curl size.
- **Afro:** shape (round, tapered, high top), length in cm, edges.
- **Sponge curls / twists:** small defined coils on top, usually with a taper or fade.
- **360 waves:** short brushed hair in concentric waves around the crown.
- **Edgar:** straight blunt fringe line, high fade or skin taper.

Curly lengths are judged dry, and curls shrink. Say whether a length is stretched or as it sits.

## Front and hairline

- **Fringe:** none (forehead open), short textured, blunt straight, curtain.
- **Line-up / shape-up:** sharp edges at the forehead and temples. Natural hairline: left as it grows.
- **Part:** none, natural, side part (which side), hard part.

## Finish

- **Matte:** no shine. **Natural:** slight sheen. **Wet look / slick:** high shine.
- **Textured, piece-y:** separated defined pieces, some spikes.
- **Tousled, bedhead:** loose, random, undone.
- **Smooth:** pieces lie together, no separation.

## Ambiguous words

Confirm these with options the first time a user says them, then record the meaning in their profile.

| Word | Possible meanings |
|---|---|
| Messy | Textured and piece-y; tousled bedhead; disconnected and choppy |
| Clean | Short with crisp lines; neat but the same length; sharp line-up |
| Volume | Height at the front; fullness and width; looks thicker overall |
| Short | Short sides only; short all over; just a trim |
| Natural | No product; styled to look unstyled; no visible fade |
| Edgy | High-contrast fade; designs or a hard part; unusual length or shape |
| Sharp | Crisp lines and edges; pointed spikes |
| Fresh | Just-cut look; a change from what they have |
